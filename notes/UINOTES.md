## Profit & Waste Analytics Section

# Ingredient Cost Trends
- Contents
    - Line chart by ingredient (see trends over time)
    - Table recent price changes (spot supplier shifts or inflation)
    - Vendor comparision (which vendor charges more per item)
    - top 5 risers/fallers (auto highlight major changes)
# Dish Profitability
- Contents
    - Bar chart: profit per dish (see best and worst performers)
    - Table: cogs %, sale price (drill into item level breakdown)
    - margin tiers: (green = > 70%, yell = 40-70%, red < 40%)
    - filter by category(appetizers, deserts, etc)

# Waste Dashboard
- Contents
    - pie chart: type (spoilage overprep, returned?) breakdown by cause
    - Bar chart: Waste by dish or category (identify high waste items)
    - waste trends over time: know if training or changes helped
    - logs table: date, item,reason,quantity

# Insights & Optimization
- Contents
    - "Dish a is high cost, low volume cut it?" data-backed menu sugg
    - "supplier X has 12% high avg. costs" negotiate or switch vendors
    - "reduce prep batch for dish b on wed" minimize overproduction
    - "Labor vs profit misaligned last week" cross-reference staff cost data
